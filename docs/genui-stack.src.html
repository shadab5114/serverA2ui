One request, end to end

  1. The AG-UI client POSTs the prompt or a userAction to /agui/run, with the persona in state, and keeps an SSE stream open.
  2. FastAPI passes a RunAgentInput to LangGraph, and the checkpointer restores the thread from Postgres.
  3. Decide works out what’s needed and whether a curated template covers it. Policy applies personas.json and falls back if this persona isn’t allowed that strategy.
  4. TEXT streams straight out. TEMPLATE loads a curated template and fills it from data providers. ADAPT, GENERATE and REFINE first Ground through your MCP servers and RAG endpoint, producing a cited design brief.
  5. Build fills, patches or composes. Verify checks the result against the schema and the guidelines, and repairs failures or asks the designer.
  6. The AG-UI encoder streams events back. The client sends them to the chat, gallery and trace panel, and the A2UI renderer draws with PDS components.
